Count and Noncount Nouns 3

Count nouns have a singular and a plural form. Noncount nouns have only one form.

Grammar Notes

1. Count nouns

You can count them: one, two, three ...

a desk, two desks  ·  a coat, three coats

2. Noncount nouns

You can't count them. They have no plural form. Don't use a or an.

heat, wax, peace (not a heat, peaces)

3. A, an, or some

  • a + singular count noun (consonant sound): a desk
  • an + singular count noun (vowel sound): an ant
  • some + plural count noun: some desks
  • some + noncount noun: some wax

4. Is or are

Singular count noun or noncount noun + is. Plural count noun + are.

The desk is big. The desks are big. The wax is soft.

5. Container and amount words

Use a container or amount word + of.

a bag of oranges, two cups of tea, two pounds of shrimp

6. Much, many, a few, a little

  • many / a few + plural count noun: How many desks? a few desks
  • much / a little + noncount noun: How much wax? a little wax

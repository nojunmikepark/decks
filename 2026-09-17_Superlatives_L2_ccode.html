Superlatives

the -est and the most

Level 2 — Grammar

 

The Idea: Three or More

Rex weighs 10 pounds. Max weighs 40 pounds. Duke weighs 90 pounds.
2 dogs: Max is heavier than Rex.  (comparative)
3 or more dogs: Duke is the heaviest dog.  (superlative)

A superlative says who or what is number 1 in a group. Always use the.

 

Short Adjectives: the + -est

AdjectiveRuleExample
1 syllableadd -esttall → the tallest
ends in -eadd -ststrange → the strangest
ends in 1 vowel + 1 consonantdouble the last letter + -estthin → the thinnest
2 syllables, ends in -ychange y to i + -estcrazy → the craziest
Watch out: the tallest ✓   tallest ✗ (no the)   the taller ✗ (for 3 or more)
 

Long Adjectives and Irregulars

2 or more syllables (no -y): the most + adjective
famous → the most famous   important → the most important
Irregular: a new word
good → the best   bad → the worst
in + a place or group: the tallest in my family
of + a number or all: the tallest of the four
Watch out: never use most and -est together. the most famous ✓   the most famousest ✗

Exercise 2 — Make the Superlative

Say the sentence with the word in ( ). Then click to check.

1. (quick) The train is ____ way to get downtown.

the quickest — 1 syllable: the + -est.

2. (hot) Which pepper on the plate is ____?

the hottest — 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the t.

3. (lucky) Who is ____ person you know?

the luckiest — Ends in -y: the luckiest.

4. (exciting) What is ____ sport to watch?

the most exciting — Long adjective: the most.

5. (good) Where is ____ taco place near here?

the best — Irregular: good → the best.

6. (close) What is ____ store to your home?

the closest — Ends in -e: add -st.

 

Exercise 3 — Find the Mistake

The CAPITALIZED word or words are wrong. Say the correct word or words. Then click to check.

1. Who is the FUNNYEST actor on TV?

funniest — Ends in -y: change y to i + -est.

2. That is the BEAUTIFULEST song.

most beautiful — Long adjective: the most, no -est.

3. This is the REDEST apple in the bowl.

reddest — 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the d.

4. Friday has the WORSE traffic of the week.

worst — Irregular: bad → the worst.

5. The MOST GOOD part of the trip was the food.

best — Irregular: good → the best.

 

Exercise 4 — in or of?

Say in or of. Then click to check.

1. Mr. Lee is the oldest teacher ____ our school.

in — a place or group (our school) → in.

2. This bag is the lightest ____ the three.

of — a number (the three) → of.

3. My mom is the busiest person ____ our house.

in — a place (our house) → in.

4. Rain is the worst weather ____ all.

of — all → of.

5. Jake is the strongest ____ the five brothers.

of — a number (the five brothers) → of.

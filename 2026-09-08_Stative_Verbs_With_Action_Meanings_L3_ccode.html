Stative Verbs With Action Meanings

Why "I think it is a good idea" but "I am thinking about dinner"

Level 3 — Grammar

 

State or Action?

A STATE is something that is true: an opinion, a feeling, a fact, or what you own. You do not "do" a state. It has no start and no finish.
STATE = simple present, even with now or at the moment.
An ACTION is something you do. It starts, it happens step by step, and it ends.
ACTION happening now = am / is / are + verb-ing.
I think it is a good idea. (my opinion = a state)
I am thinking about dinner. (my mind is working right now = an action)
We have a car. (we own it = a state)
We are having a break at the moment. (have = take, do; a break starts and ends = an action)

Same word, two jobs. The meaning decides the form, not the word.

 

The Swap Test

Quick check: Try to swap the verb for a state word such as believe or own. If the swap works, it is a state: use the simple present. If the swap does not work, it is probably an action happening now: use the present continuous.
I think the movie starts at eight. → I believe the movie starts at eight. ✓ State
I am thinking about my answer. → I believe about my answer. ✗ Action
They have a big garden. → They own a big garden. ✓ State
They are having a picnic. → They own a picnic. ✗ Action
Verbs that are almost always states: know believe want need prefer own belong understand mean
 

think and have

VerbSTATE (simple present)ACTION (present continuous)
thinkbelieve, have an opinion
I think this street is dangerous.		use your mind, consider
Be quiet. I am thinking!
haveown, possess
Our school has a small library.		do an activity: have lunch, a shower, a party, a good time
The kids are having a good time at the pool.

"I am having a car" is impossible: you cannot "do" owning. "I have lunch at the moment" is wrong too: eating is an action that starts and ends.

see, look, taste, smell, feel

VerbSTATE (simple present)ACTION (present continuous)
seeunderstand, notice
I see two birds on the roof.		meet, visit
I am seeing my dentist on Friday.
lookseem, appear
She looks happy today.		point your eyes at
She is looking at the old photos.
tastehave a flavor
The coffee tastes bitter.		test the flavor
Ali is tasting the coffee.
smellgive off a smell
The pizza smells delicious.		sniff on purpose
The baker is smelling the flour.
feelhave a texture, seem
Your hands feel cold.		touch on purpose
The doctor is feeling my neck.

State meaning: the thing HAS a quality (it smells, it tastes, it feels). Action meaning: a person DOES something with a body part (a nose, a tongue, a hand, the eyes).

be and weigh

VerbSTATE (simple present)ACTION (present continuous)
becharacter, a fact
Tom is a quiet boy.		behavior only right now
Tom is being very loud today.
weighhave a weight
The box weighs three kilos.		measure the weight
The post office worker is weighing my package.
Watch out: "is being" only works for BEHAVIOR (rude, loud, silly, helpful). Age, job, size, and nationality are facts: He is a nurse. Never He is being a nurse.
 

Exercise 3 — Correct the Verb

The CAPITALIZED verb is in the wrong form. Say the correct form, then click to check.

3. This watermelon IS WEIGHING five kilos.

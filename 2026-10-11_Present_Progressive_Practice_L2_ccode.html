Present Progressive Practice

Actions happening now: am / is / are + verb-ing.

Grammar Notes

When do we use it?

For an action that is happening now. Signal words: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!

Maria is studying right now.

Positive

  • I + am + verb-ing
  • he / she / it + is + verb-ing
  • you / we / they + are + verb-ing

Short forms: I'm, he's, she's, it's, you're, we're, they're

They're riding the train.

Negative

am not / is not (isn't) / are not (aren't) + verb-ing

It isn't raining.

Yes/No questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?

Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.  Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.  Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Do not use a short form in a "Yes" answer: say "Yes, it is." not "Yes, it's."

Is Maria studying? Yes, she is.

Wh- questions

Wh- word + am / is / are + subject + verb-ing?

Where is Maria studying?

Spelling: verb + -ing

  • Most verbs: add -ing (rain → raining)
  • Verb ends in a silent -e: drop the e (ride → riding). Not -ee or -ie (free → freeing, tie → tying).
  • One-syllable verb that ends in consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter (stop → stopping). Not w, x or y (show → showing).
  • Verb ends in -ie: change ie to y (tie → tying)

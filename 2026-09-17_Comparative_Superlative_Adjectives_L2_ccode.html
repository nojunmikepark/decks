Comparative and Superlative Adjectives

-er than, the -est, more, the most

Level 2 — Grammar

 

Two Things or Three or More?

Monday: 70°F.   Tuesday: 80°F.   Wednesday: 90°F.
2 days: Tuesday is warmer than Monday.  (comparative)
3 or more days: Wednesday is the warmest day.  (superlative)

Comparative: use than. Superlative: use the.

 

How to Make Them

AdjectiveComparative (2 things)Superlative (3 or more)
1 syllablecold → colder thanthe coldest
ends in -esafe → safer thanthe safest
1 vowel + 1 consonanthot → hotter thanthe hottest
2 syllables, ends in -yeasy → easier thanthe easiest
2 or more syllables (no -y)famous → more famous thanthe most famous
irregulargood → better than
bad → worse than
far → farther than		the best
the worst
the farthest
exceptionstired → more tired than
bored → more bored than		the most tired
the most bored
Watch out: never use more with -er or most with -est. more colder ✗   the most coldest ✗

Exercise 2 — Comparative or Superlative?

Say the sentence with the word in ( ). Then click to check.

1. (slow) My old computer is ____ than my new one.

slower — than = 2 things. 1 syllable: add -er.

2. (crowded) Saturday is ____ day at the mall.

the most crowded — 3 or more days. Long adjective: the most.

3. (large) My new desk is ____ than my old desk.

larger — than = 2 things. Ends in -e: add -r.

4. (happy) Friday is ____ day of the week for me.

the happiest — day of the week = 3 or more. Ends in -y: the -iest.

5. (far) Which store is ____ from here: Target, Costco, or Walmart?

the farthest (or the furthest) — Three stores. Irregular: far → the farthest.

6. (sunny) Arizona is ____ than Oregon.

sunnier — than = 2 things. Ends in -y: change y to i + -er.

Count and Noncount Nouns 2

Count nouns have a singular and a plural form. Noncount nouns have only one form.

Grammar Notes

1. Count nouns

You can count them: one, two, three ...

a hat, two hats  ·  a door, three doors

2. Noncount nouns

You can't count them. They have no plural form. Don't use a or an.

oil, gold, fun (not an oil, funs)

3. A, an, or some

  • a + singular count noun (consonant sound): a hat
  • an + singular count noun (vowel sound): an ear
  • some + plural count noun: some hats
  • some + noncount noun: some oil

4. Is or are

Singular count noun or noncount noun + is. Plural count noun + are.

The hat is new. The hats are new. The oil is hot.

5. Container and amount words

Use a container or amount word + of.

a bag of chips, two pounds of grapes, a tube of lipstick

6. Much, many, a few, a little

  • many / a few + plural count noun: How many hats? a few hats
  • much / a little + noncount noun: How much oil? a little oil

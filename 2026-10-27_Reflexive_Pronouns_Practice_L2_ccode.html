Reflexive Pronouns

myself, yourself, himself … · Level 2

 

The Reflexive Pronouns

SubjectReflexiveExample
ImyselfI asked myself a question.
you (one person)yourselfYou can pay for your lunch yourself.
hehimselfHe saw himself on TV.
sheherselfShe went to the movies by herself.
ititselfThe robot can charge itself.
weourselvesWe planned the party ourselves.
you (two or more)yourselvesYou and Tom can wash the dishes yourselves.
theythemselvesThey found the answer themselves.

One person or thing: -self. Two or more: -selves.

 

Use 1: The Same Person

Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and the object are the same person or thing.

Carlos washed him.  → Carlos washed a different man.
Carlos washed himself.  → Carlos washed Carlos.
Quick check: Is the object the same person as the subject? Yes → use -self or -selves.

Use 2: Emphasis · Use 3: by + -self

Emphasis: that person did it, not someone else.
The mayor called me herself!
by + reflexive pronoun = alone, or with no help.
My grandpa goes fishing by himself.

Practice 3: Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

1. My mom changed the flat tire HERSELVES.

My mom changed the flat tire herself. — One person → -self: herself.

2. The players pushed HIMSELF to run faster.

The players pushed themselves to run faster. — The players = they → themselves.

3. I signed ME up for a dance class.

I signed myself up for a dance class. — The subject (I) and the object are the same person → myself.

4. Kids, you can choose the music YOURSELF.

Kids, you can choose the music yourselves. — Two or more people → yourselves.

5. The toy car moved HERSELF across the floor.

The toy car moved itself across the floor. — A thing = it → itself.

6. Kevin made HERSELF a smoothie.

Kevin made himself a smoothie. — Kevin = he → himself.

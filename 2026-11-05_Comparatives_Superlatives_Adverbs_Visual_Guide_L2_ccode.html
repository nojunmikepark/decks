Practice 4 · 1–2

Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

1. The gray kitten is MORE CUTER than the white kitten.

The gray kitten is cuter than the white kitten. — Use -er OR more, not both.

2. Paris is LARGEST city in France.

Paris is the largest city in France. — A superlative needs the.

 

Practice 4 · 3–4

Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

3. Lin drives MORE FAST than Ivan.

Lin drives faster than Ivan. — fast is the same word as the adjective: add -er.

4. This movie is SADER than the book.

This movie is sadder than the book. — sad ends in consonant + vowel + consonant: double the d.

 

Practice 4 · 5–6

Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

5. Mount Whitney is THE MOST HIGHEST mountain in California.

Mount Whitney is the highest mountain in California. — Use -est OR most, not both.

6. Yuki speaks MORE HONEST than Bruno.

Yuki speaks more honestly than Bruno. — speaks is an action: adverb.

Level 3 — Grammar

 

Connectors Join Ideas

A connector joins two ideas. Each connector has a different job.

and = add more  ·  but = contrast  ·  so = result
because / because of = reason

Ask: Am I adding, showing a surprise, giving a result, or giving a reason?

 

and · but · so

and adds information.
She plays the piano and sings in a choir.
but shows a contrast (a different or surprising idea).
The box is heavy, but I can carry it.
so shows a result.
I was very tired, so I went to bed early.
Comma: put a comma before but and so when they join two complete sentences.
 

because vs. because of

because + subject + verb
Ana took a taxi because it was raining.
because of + noun
Ana took a taxi because of the rain.
Quick check: Is there a verb after it? Yes → because. No verb, only a noun → because of.

so introduces the result. because introduces the reason.

Exercise 2 — because or because of?

Say the answer first, then click to check.

1. The baby is crying ____ she is hungry.

because — she is = subject + verb.

2. We ate dinner outside ____ the beautiful weather.

because of — the beautiful weather = a noun, no verb.

3. My eyes hurt ____ the bright sun.

because of — the bright sun = a noun.

4. Paul was late for work ____ he overslept.

because — he overslept = subject + verb.

5. I drink tea at night ____ coffee keeps me awake.

because — coffee keeps = subject + verb.

6. The kids couldn’t sleep ____ the thunder.

because of — the thunder = a noun.

 

Exercise 3 — Fix the Mistake

The CAPITALIZED word or words are wrong. Say the correct connector, then click to check.

1. Jenny got a headache BECAUSE the loud music.

because of — the loud music is a noun.

2. It was sunny all day, SO the water in the pool was freezing.

but — Freezing water on a sunny day is a surprise. Use but for a contrast.

3. Tom stayed home from work BECAUSE OF he had a fever.

because — he had = subject + verb.

4. I need to buy shampoo, toothpaste, BUT soap.

and — This is a list. Use and before the last item.

5. We couldn’t hear the teacher BECAUSE the noise outside.

because of — the noise outside is a noun.

6. The kitchen smelled great BECAUSE OF my mom was baking cookies.

because — my mom was baking = subject + verb.

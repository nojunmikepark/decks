Possessive Adjectives and Pronouns

Possessive words tell us who owns something.

Grammar Notes

1. Possessive adjectives

A possessive adjective comes before a noun.

my chair, her plant, their garage

2. Possessive pronouns

A possessive pronoun stands alone. No noun comes after it.

The chair is mine. The plant is hers.

4. Whose?

Use whose to ask who owns something.

Whose plants are these? They're Jun's.

5. Its or it's?

its = possessive. it's = it is.

The tree lost its leaves. It's cold today.

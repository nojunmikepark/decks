Practice 3 · 1–2

Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

1. The boy answered the question HONEST.

The boy answered the question honestly. — answered = action → adverb.

2. This pillow feels SOFTLY.

This pillow feels soft. — feels + adjective. It describes the pillow.

Level 3 — Grammar Review

 

Two Parts, Two Places

A phrasal verb has two parts: a verb + a particle (up, out, off, on, in, back, over, away).

Noun: the noun can go in the middle or at the end.
throw away the box = throw the box away

Pronoun (me, you, him, her, it, us, them): the pronoun MUST go in the middle.
throw it away ✓    throw away it ✗

With get up, put the person in the middle: get the kids up.

give back = return to a PERSON. bring back = return to a PLACE.

Exercise 2 — Use the Correct Form

Say the verb in parentheses in the correct form. If there is a pronoun, put it in the correct place. Then click to check.

1. Have you ______ your vacation request yet? (hand in)

handed in — have + past participle.

2. Last night, my son finally ______ the last level of his video game. (figure out)

figured out — "Last night" = simple past.

3. Right now, the technician ______ the new smart board in our classroom. (hook up)

is hooking up — "Right now" = present continuous.

4. Every Sunday night, the kids ______ the recycling bins from the street. (bring back)

bring back — "The kids" is plural, so no -s.

5. My grandmother can't see well, so my mother always ______ her forms at the bank. (fill out)

fills out — "Always" + one person = simple present with -s.

6. Last night, I ______ the car lights, and this morning the battery was dead. (leave on)

left on — Leave → left (irregular past).

7. My husband wanted to sleep late, but the plumber came at 8:00, so I ______. (get up / him)

got him up — Past tense, and the pronoun goes in the middle.

8. My handwriting on this birthday card is messy. I'm going to ______ the card. (do over)

do over — "Going to" + base form.

 

Exercise 3 — Use a Pronoun

Change the CAPITALIZED words to it, them, him, or her. Say the new sentence, then click to check.

1. Can you pick up MY SISTER after her dance class?

Can you pick her up after her dance class?

2. Please put away THE PAINT BRUSHES when you finish.

Please put them away when you finish.

3. I called up MR. OKAFOR about the broken window.

I called him up about the broken window.

4. Did you fill out THE CUSTOMS CARD on the plane?

Did you fill it out on the plane?

5. Kevin gave back THE MONEY to his brother.

Kevin gave it back to his brother.

6. Did the electrician hook up THE OUTDOOR LIGHTS?

Did the electrician hook them up?

 

Exercise 4 — Mixed Practice: Fix the Mistake

Each CAPITALIZED part has one mistake. Say the correct words, then click to check.

1. Every year, my students PICKS OUT a class song, and they sing it at graduation.

pick out — "My students" is plural.

2. Last night, nobody PUTTED AWAY the ice cream, so it melted.

put away — Put → put (the past is the same).

3. The school bus DROPS OUT the children at 3:15.

drops off — Drop off = take someone somewhere and leave.

4. Tomorrow we will HUNG UP the new mirror in the bathroom.

hang up — Will + base form.

5. My phone was broken, but yesterday my brother FIGURES OUT what was wrong.

figured out — "Yesterday" = simple past.

6. What does this road sign mean? Let's LOOK UP IT in the driver's handbook.

look it up — The pronoun goes in the middle.

Grammar · Level 3 · Gerunds

Adjective / Verb + Preposition + -ing

afraid of, good at, thank you for, think about

preposition + verb-ing
 

Part 1 · The idea

adjective + preposition

Many adjectives go with one preposition (of, about, at, for, in, with).

After the preposition, use a noun or a verb + -ing (a gerund).

Mateo is afraid of spiders. (noun)
Mateo is afraid of touching spiders. (verb + -ing)
My aunt is good at typing fast.
We are excited about camping at the lake.
be + adjective + preposition + verb-ing

Part 2 · Verbs

verb + preposition

Some verbs go with a preposition, too. Use verb + -ing after them.

My grandpa is thinking about retiring.
Lately, I've been thinking about jogging in the morning.
Thank you for coming!
She thanked me for helping.
think about + verb-ing   thank (someone) for + verb-ing
 

Part 2 · Watch out

-ing, never to + verb

After a preposition, use verb + -ing. Do not use to + verb or the base verb.

Wrong

Ella is tired of to travel for work.

Ella is tired of travel for work.

Right

Ella is tired of traveling for work.

Tip: about, of, at, for, in, with + -ing.

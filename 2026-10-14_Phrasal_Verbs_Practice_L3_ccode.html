Phrasal Verbs

A verb + a small word (up, out, off …) that together have a new meaning. Some can split around the object. Some cannot.

Grammar Notes

1. Separable phrasal verbs

A noun can go after the small word or in the middle.

A pronoun (it, them, him, her, me, us, you) must go in the middle.

Try on the shoes. = Try the shoes on.
Try them on. (not: Try on them.)

2. Inseparable phrasal verbs

The object always goes after the small word, noun or pronoun.

We went over the plan. We went over it.
(not: We went the plan over.)

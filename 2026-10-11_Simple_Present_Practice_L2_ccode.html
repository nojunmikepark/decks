Simple Present

Habits, facts, and schedules. With he, she, and it, the verb ends in -s.

Grammar Notes

1. Positive sentences

I / you / we / they + verb

he / she / it + verb + -s

I drink tea. She drinks tea.

2. Negative sentences

I / you / we / they + don't + verb

he / she / it + doesn't + verb (no -s)

They don't drink soda. He doesn't drink soda.

3. Questions

Do + I / you / we / they + verb?

Does + he / she / it + verb (no -s)?

Do you drink milk? Yes, I do.
Does she drink milk? No, she doesn't.
What does he drink? He drinks juice.

4. When do we use it?

  • Habits (things we do again and again): every day, on weekends, in the morning, always, usually, often, sometimes, never
  • Facts: The sun rises in the east.
  • Schedules: Our train departs at 8:15.

5. Spelling with he / she / it

  • Most verbs: + s (buy → buys)
  • -s, -sh, -ch, -x: + es (pass → passes, mix → mixes)
  • go → goes
  • consonant + y: y → ies (reply → replies)
  • have → has

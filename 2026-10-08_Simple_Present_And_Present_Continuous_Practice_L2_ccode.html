Simple Present and Present Continuous

Habits and routines, or actions happening now. Look for the time words.

Grammar Notes

Simple present: habits and routines

I / you / we / they + verb. He / she / it + verb + -s.

Negative: don't / doesn't + verb. Question: Do / Does + subject + verb?

  • every day, on Mondays, at night
  • always, usually, often, sometimes, never

Ken delivers packages every weekday.

Present continuous: happening now

am / is / are + verb + -ing.

Negative: am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing. Question: Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?

  • now, right now, at the moment
  • Look! Listen! Shh!

Right now, Ken is parking his van.

Stative Verbs (Non-Action Verbs)

Some verbs are about thinking, feeling or owning, not doing. Use the simple present for them, even with now.

  • know, believe, remember, belong, own, hate

I know his name now. (NOT: I am knowing)

-er / more … than  ·  not as … as  ·  the -est / the most

Level 2 — Grammar Review

 

Core Idea — Two Things or Three Things?

2 things: -er than or more … than
2 things, the other way: not as … as
3 or more things: the -est or the most …
A dog is bigger than a cat.
A cat is not as big as a dog.
Soccer is the most exciting sport for me.

Look for the clue: than = two things. in the world, in my family, of all = three or more.

Not As … As — Say It the Other Way

Rule: Switch the two things. Use the simple adjective between not as and as.
No -er. No more.
Maria's street is noisier than my street.
→ My street is not as noisy as Maria's street.
Soccer is more exciting than golf.
→ Golf is not as exciting as soccer.

The meaning does not change. Only the order changes.

Three or More — The -est / The Most

Rule: Compare one thing with a whole group. Always use the.
the + adjective-est  or  the most + long adjective
Maria's street is the noisiest street in town.
Friday is the best day of the week.

Clue words: in the world, in my family, in the class, of the year, of all.

Exercise 5 — Fix the Mistake

The CAPITALIZED word or words are wrong. Say the correct words. Then click to check.

1. This bag is MORE BIGGER than that bag.

bigger — Use only one form. No more with -er.

2. Sunday is the SLOWER day of the week at my store.

slowest — the days of the week = a group → the slowest.

3. My bedroom is not as warm THAN the living room.

as — not as warm as.

4. Julia is the MOST GOOD cook in our family.

best — good → better → the best.

5. A plane is FASTEST than a train.

faster — than = two things → faster.

6. This is the MOST EASY recipe in the cookbook.

easiest — easy ends in -y → the easiest.

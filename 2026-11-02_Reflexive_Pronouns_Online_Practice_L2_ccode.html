Reflexive Pronouns

A reflexive pronoun ends in -self or -selves.

Grammar Notes

1. The forms

SubjectReflexive
Imyself
you (one person)yourself
hehimself
sheherself
ititself
weourselves
you (two or more)yourselves
theythemselves

One person or thing: -self. Two or more: -selves.

2. Same person

Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and the object are the same person or thing.

Mia smiled at herself. (Mia = herself)

Mia smiled at her. (her = another woman)

3. No help

A reflexive pronoun can also mean nobody helped.

We planted the tree ourselves.

4. by + reflexive

It means alone, or nobody helped.

Leo lives by himself.

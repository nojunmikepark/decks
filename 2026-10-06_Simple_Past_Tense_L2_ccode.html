Simple Past Tense

regular verbs, irregular verbs, negatives, and questions

Level 2 — Grammar Review

 

When Do We Use the Simple Past?

Use the simple past for an action that started and finished in the past.

Time words: yesterday · last night · last week · two days ago · in 2022
Nina painted her bedroom last week.
We visited Chicago in 2022.

The simple past form is the same for every subject: I, you, he, she, we, they. (Except be: was / were.)

 

Regular Verbs: -ed Spelling

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -edjump → jumped
Verb ends in -e: add -dsmile → smiled
Consonant + y: change y to i, add -edcopy → copied
Vowel + y: just add -edstay → stayed
One-syllable verb, consonant + vowel + consonant (not w, x, or y): double the last letterchat → chatted
 

Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs do not use -ed. Learn each past form.

Leo sang at his friend's birthday dinner.
 

Negatives and Questions

Negative: didn't (did not) + base verb
Question: Did + subject + base verb?
I didn't sleep well last night.
Did you hear the news? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Watch out: I didn't knew. → I didn't know.

Exercise 2 — Write the Past Form

Write the simple past of the verb in parentheses. Then click to check.

1. Yesterday, Ines ____ all the windows. (wash)

washed — regular: add -ed.

2. Last spring, Ivan ____ for a new job. (apply)

applied — consonant + y: change y to i, add -ed.

3. My grandfather ____ in the army in 1975. (serve)

served — ends in -e: add -d.

4. Last week, Ali fell and ____ his arm. (break)

broke — irregular: break → broke.

5. We ____ the news on TV last night. (not / watch)

didn't watch (or did not watch) — didn't + base verb.

6. On Friday, Kim ____ a long blue dress to the wedding. (wear)

wore — irregular: wear → wore.

7. Last Tuesday, my manager ____ me a raise. (give)

gave — irregular: give → gave.

8. ____ you ____ your passport at home last night? (forget)

Did … forget — Did + subject + base verb.

 

Exercise 3 — Fix the Mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Correct the CAPITALIZED word. Then click to check.

1. Last weekend, Hamid BRINGED a big salad to the picnic.

brought — Bring is irregular: bring → brought.

2. Ray JOGED around the school track yesterday.

jogged — One-syllable verb, consonant + vowel + consonant: double the g.

3. Did the plane LANDED on time?

land — After Did, use the base verb.

4. Amir didn't FELT well yesterday.

feel — After didn't, use the base verb.

5. Last week, Ella SPEAKED to the manager about her hours.

spoke — Speak is irregular: speak → spoke.

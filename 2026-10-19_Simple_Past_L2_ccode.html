The Simple Past

time markers, regular verbs, irregular verbs, and be

Level 2 — Grammar Review

 

When Do We Use the Simple Past?

Use the simple past for an action that started and finished in the past.

Time markers tell when the action happened.
Time markerExample
yesterdayI rented a car yesterday.
lastWe ordered Thai food last night.
agoShe mailed the package two hours ago.
whenHe folded the laundry when he got home.
in / on / atThey planted a tree in May.
We met on Friday at noon.

in + year or month · on + day · at + clock time

Regular Verbs: -ed Spelling

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -edrent → rented
Ends in -e: add -drinse → rinsed
Consonant + y: change y to i, add -edbury → buried
Vowel + y: add -edobey → obeyed
One syllable, consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter, add -ednod → nodded
Watch out: do not double w, x, or y (relax → relaxed). Two syllables: double only when the last part is stressed (adMIT → admitted, OPen → opened). Exceptions: pay → paid, say → said.

Negatives and Questions: did

Negative: didn't (did not) + base verb
Question: Did + subject + base verb?
Wh- question: Wh- word + did + subject + base verb?
I didn't rent a car.
Did you fold the towels? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Where did they order the pizza?
Watch out: after did and didn't, use the base verb. I didn't rented. → I didn't rent.

Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs do not use -ed. The word changes, or it stays the same. Learn each past form.

BasePastBasePast
knowknewswimswam
standstoodwritewrote
putputsleepslept
I knew the answer.
We stood near the window.

Negatives and questions are the same as regular verbs: I didn't know. Did you write it?

The Verb Be: was / were

SubjectPositiveNegative
I / he / she / it / one thingwaswasn't (was not)
you / we / they / two or more thingswereweren't (were not)
The parking lot was full. The kittens were tiny.
Was the hotel clean? — Yes, it was. · Where were the keys?
Watch out: do not use did with be. Did the hotel was clean? → Was the hotel clean?

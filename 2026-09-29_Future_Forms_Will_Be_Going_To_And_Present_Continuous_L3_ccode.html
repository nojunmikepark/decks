Future Forms: Will, Be Going To, and Present Continuous

Three ways to talk about the future

Level 3 — Grammar Review

 

Three Ways to Talk About the Future

English has three common future forms. Each one has its own job.

FormMain job
will + verbdecision now, offer, promise, opinion
be going to + verbplan (decided before now), evidence
am / is / are + verb-ingarrangement (a fixed plan: set up with other people or at a set time)

Ask: When did I decide? Can I see it coming? Is it on my calendar?

 

will + verb

Form: will + base verb  •  won't + base verb  •  Will + subject + base verb?
Decision now: The door is locked? I'll get the key.
Offer: You cooked. I'll wash the dishes.
Promise: I won't be late again.
Opinion: I think you'll love this book.
Watch out: no to and no -s after will. She will go. (not will to go, not will goes)
 

be going to + verb

Form: am / is / are + going to + base verb
Plan: We're going to repaint the living room next month.
Evidence: The score is 7 to 0. Our team is going to win.
Negative: He isn't going to change jobs.
Question: Are they going to buy a new TV?
Watch out: keep be. She is going to call. (not She going to call)
 

am / is / are + verb-ing

Form: am / is / are + verb-ing + a future time
Arrangement: I'm flying to Seattle on Tuesday. I have my ticket.
Arrangement: Jin and Rosa are getting married in June.
Question: Are you working this Saturday?
Watch out: do not use it for weather or for be. It's being cold next week ✗  →  It will be cold next week. ✓
 

Plans: going to or -ing?

For plans, both forms are often correct.

I'm going to see my aunt on Sunday. = I'm seeing my aunt on Sunday.

-ing sounds more fixed: the plan is set up with other people or at a set time.

going to works for any plan, even with no time.

Someday, I'm going to write a book. ✓    Someday, I'm writing a book. ✗

Exercise 3 — Fill in the Blank

Say the future form of the verb in parentheses. Then click to check.

1. "My pen doesn't work." "Here, I ______ (lend) you mine."

'll lend (will lend) — an offer, decided now.

2. Jamal ______ (start) a new class on Monday. He registered online.

is starting (or is going to start) — an arrangement with a day.

3. Watch out! That cup ______ (fall) off the edge.

is going to fall (or will fall) — evidence: you can see it now.

4. I ______ (not / forget) your birthday this year. I promise.

won't forget (or will not forget / am not going to forget) — a promise.

5. What ______ (you / wear) to the job interview on Thursday?

are you wearing (or are you going to wear) — an arrangement with a day.

6. I think it ______ (be) a long winter.

will be (or is going to be) — an opinion with "I think".

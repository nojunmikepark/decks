Separable Phrasal Verbs (13–19)

hook up · leave on · look up · pick up · pick out · put off · put away

Level 3 — Grammar

 

What Does "Separable" Mean?

A separable phrasal verb has two parts: a verb + a particle (up, on, off, out, away).

Noun: two places are OK.
Hook up the microwave. = Hook the microwave up.

Pronoun (it, them, him, her): the pronoun MUST go in the middle.
Hook it up. ✓    Hook up it. ✗

Say the pronoun between the two parts: verb + it + particle.

 

13. hook up

Meaning: connect the wires on a machine or appliance
My neighbor hooked up our new internet router on Monday.
The new security camera was easy. My son hooked it up before dinner.
 

14. leave on

Meaning: not turn off (an appliance or machine)
Please leave the air conditioner on for the dog.
The coffee maker was still hot at noon because I left it on.
 

15. look up

Meaning: try to find in a book or on the Internet
I looked up the store hours before I drove there.
I forgot the name of that actor. I'll look it up.
 

16. pick up

Meaning: stop to get someone
My husband picks up the kids every Friday.
My mother doesn't drive, so I pick her up for church on Sundays.
 

17. pick out

Meaning: select or choose something
Let's pick out a movie for tonight.
Which tie should I wear? Please pick one out for me.
 

18. put off

Meaning: postpone or delay something
Don't put off your visit to the doctor.
I need to do my taxes, but I keep putting them off.
 

19. put away

Meaning: return something to its proper place
Put away your phone during the test.
The board games are on the table. Can you put them away?

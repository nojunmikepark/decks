Grammar · Level 2 · Modals 1

Be Able To, Could & Might

What people can do, and what is possible

modal + base verb
 

Part 1 · Ability

be able to

Use be able to to say a person can do something.

TimeFormExample
Nowam / is / are able toNadia is able to sew.
Pastwas / were able toWe were able to sell the car.
Futurewill be able toI will be able to knit soon.
Negativeisn't / wasn't able toNadia isn't able to knit.
Question: Am / Is / Are / Was / Were + subject + able to + verb?   Will + subject + be able to + verb?
Watch out: be changes. able to does not change.
 

Part 2 · Past ability

could / couldn't

Use could for something a person was able to do in the past.

When I was a teenager, I could jump very high.
Pavel couldn't whistle as a child.
Question: Could + subject + base verb?   Could Pavel whistle?
Watch out: could never changes, and we do not use to after it.
 

Part 3 · Maybe

might / might not

Use might when you are not sure. It means maybe.

Pat might be sick today.
That key might not fit the lock.

Might never changes: I might, you might, he might, they might.

 

Part 4 · Maybe

could = maybe

could has a second meaning. It can also mean maybe.

That jacket could be expensive.
Mina could be at home.
Watch out: for the negative, use might not.

Gerunds and Infinitives

Some verbs take a gerund (-ing). Some take an infinitive (to + verb). Some take both.

Grammar Notes

1. Verb + gerund (-ing)

enjoy, avoid, finish, deny, dislike, postpone, mind, suggest …

I don't mind waiting.
They suggested meeting downtown.

2. Verb + infinitive (to + verb)

want, would like, need, decide, promise, agree, offer, hope …

She hopes to win the race.

3. Verb + person + to + verb

ask, want, expect, need, advise, tell, remind …

Dad reminded me to lock the gate.

4. Both forms, same meaning

begin, start, continue, like, love, hate, can't stand

He hates waiting. = He hates to wait.

5. Negatives

not + -ing, or not to + verb (also to not + verb)

I suggest not eating late.
He hopes not to miss the bus.

Part 1 · The idea

A verb + a small word

A phrasal verb is a verb + a small word (up, out, off, back, down). Together they have a new meaning.

Many phrasal verbs are separable: a noun can go at the end or in the middle.

Please turn off the lights.
Please turn the lights off.

Both sentences are correct. They mean the same thing.

 

Part 2 · Pronouns

A pronoun goes in the middle

ObjectAt the endIn the middle
a noun: the box✓ I threw away the box.✓ I threw the box away.
a pronoun: it✗ I threw away it.✓ I threw it away.
Watch out: with me, you, him, her, it, us, them, the pronoun MUST go in the middle.
She put on her coat. → She put it on.

Part 4 · Verb forms

Change the verb, not the small word

Only the first word changes for tense. The small word stays the same.

The alarm wakes me up at 6:00. (now, every day)
The alarm woke me up at 6:00. (past)
Some past forms are irregular: wake → woke   throw → threw

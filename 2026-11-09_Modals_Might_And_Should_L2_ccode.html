Grammar · Level 2 · Modals

May, Might, Could, and Should

maybe · a good idea · a bad idea

modal + base verb
 

Part 1 · Maybe

may / might

Use may or might when something is possible, but you are not sure.

The package might arrive today.
The price may go up.
Watch out: may and might never add -s, and we do not use to after them.
 

Part 2 · Maybe not

may not / might not

may not and might not mean maybe not. Put not after the modal.

This store might not carry my size.
Form: may not / might not + base verb
 

Part 3 · Maybe

could

could can also mean maybe. Use it for now or later.

That could be the new teacher.
Watch out: could not does not mean maybe not. Say may not or might not.
 

Part 4 · A good idea

should

Use should to give advice. It means it is a good idea.

You should see this movie.
Watch out: should never adds -s, and we do not use to after it.
 

Part 5 · A bad idea and questions

shouldn't / Should ...?

UseFormExample
a bad ideashouldn't + base verb
(shouldn't = should not)		You shouldn't skip breakfast.
asking for adviceShould + subject + base verb?Should I turn left here?

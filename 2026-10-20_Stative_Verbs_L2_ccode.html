Stative Verbs

States and Actions

Level 2 — Grammar

 

Two Kinds of Verbs

Action verbsStative verbs
things people dofeelings, thoughts, owning, senses
run, cook, drive, swim, writelove, think, have, know, need
Ruth is cooking pasta right now.Ruth loves pasta.

A state is not an action. You cannot "watch" someone love or know something.

 

Stative Verb Groups

Feelings: like, love, hate, prefer
Thinking: know, think (= opinion), understand, remember
Owning: have, own, belong
Senses and description: see, hear, seem, look, taste, smell
Other: want, need, cost, weigh
My son loves soccer.
We have a small car.
 

The Rule: No -ing for States

Stative verbs use the simple present, even with now or right now.
Action verbs use the simple present for habits and the present continuous for now.
✗ I am thinking it is a good idea.   ✓ I think it is a good idea.
✓ Ruth cooks on Sundays.   ✓ Right now, she is cooking.
Watch out: some verbs have an action meaning too.
I have a car. (owning = state)  ·  I am having lunch. (eating = action)

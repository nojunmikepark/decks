Count and Noncount Nouns

Count nouns have a singular and a plural form. Noncount nouns have only one form.

Grammar Notes

1. Count nouns

You can count them: one, two, three ...

a book, two books  ·  a desk, three desks

2. Noncount nouns

You can't count them. They have no plural form. Don't use a or an.

milk, music, homework, money (not a milk, musics)

3. A, an, or some

  • a + singular count noun (consonant sound): a lamp
  • an + singular count noun (vowel sound): an orange
  • some + plural count noun: some cups
  • some + noncount noun: some milk

4. Is or are

Singular count noun or noncount noun + is. Plural count noun + are.

The lamp is new. The lamps are new. The milk is cold.

5. Counting noncount nouns

Use a container or amount word + of.

a cup of tea, two boxes of pasta, a slice of cake

6. Much, many, a few, a little

  • many / a few + plural count noun: How many books? a few books
  • much / a little + noncount noun: How much money? a little milk

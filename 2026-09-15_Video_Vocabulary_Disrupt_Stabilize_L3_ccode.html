Eight Action Verbs

These verbs describe a change. A picture cannot show a change, so today we use short silent clips.

VerbMeaningExample
disruptstop something from going normallyRoad work disrupted the buses all week.
stabilizemake something steady, stop it from shaking or changingThe doctors stabilized the patient.
interruptstop a person or an action for a short timePlease don't interrupt me when I'm speaking.
collapsefall down suddenlyThe old wall collapsed in the earthquake.
restorebring something back to how it wasThe city restored the old train station.
balancekeep something steady so it does not fallShe balanced a book on her head.
scattermove or throw things in many directionsThe wind scattered the papers.
supporthold something up so it does not fallTwo posts support the roof.

Past tense: all eight are regular. Add -ed (or -d): disrupted, stabilized, interrupted, collapsed, restored, balanced, scattered, supported.

Mixed Practice — Past Tense

Write the verb in the past tense. Answer first, then click to check.

1. Yesterday a thunderstorm ___ (interrupt) the soccer game for an hour.

interrupted — the game stopped for a short time and then continued.

2. Last spring, engineers ___ (stabilize) the old bridge with steel cables.

stabilized — the cables made it steady.

3. Loud music from the street ___ (disrupt) our online class this morning.

disrupted — the class could not go normally.

4. The cheap shelf ___ (collapse) when I put a heavy box on it.

collapsed — it fell down suddenly.

5. After the flood, workers ___ (restore) the water service in two days.

restored — they brought it back to normal.

6. The waiter ___ (balance) a tray of six drinks on one hand.

balanced — he kept it steady so it did not fall.

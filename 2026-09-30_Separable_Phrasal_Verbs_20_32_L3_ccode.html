Separable Phrasal Verbs (20–32)

take back · take down · take off · take out · think over · throw away · try on
turn down · turn off · turn on · use up · wake up · write down

Level 3 — Grammar

 

What Does "Separable" Mean?

A separable phrasal verb has two parts: a verb + a particle (back, down, off, out, over, away, on, up). The two parts can split: a noun or pronoun can go in the middle.

Noun: two places are OK.
Pick up the box. = Pick the box up.

Pronoun (it, them, him, her, me): the pronoun MUST go in the middle.
Pick it up. ✓    Pick up it. ✗

Say the pronoun between the two parts: verb + it + particle.

 

20. take back

Meaning: return something
Ella took back the vacuum because it was too loud.
These sunglasses broke on the first day. I'm taking them back.
 

21. take down

Meaning: remove something
After the holidays, we take down the lights on the porch.
The old map is torn. Let's take it down.
 

22. take off

Meaning: remove some article of clothing
Take off your glasses before you swim.
My scarf is too warm. I'll take it off.
 

23. take out

Meaning: remove something from inside something else
Take out your notebooks, everyone.
The towels are dry. Please take them out of the dryer.
 

24. think over

Meaning: consider something at some length
We need to think over the price of the new car.
Your idea is interesting. Let me think it over tonight.
 

25. throw away

Meaning: get rid of something; put something in the trash
Throw away the old newspapers.
This bread is moldy. Throw it away.
 

26. try on

Meaning: put clothing on to see if it fits
She tried on five hats at the market.
This belt looks nice. Can I try it on?
 

27. turn down

Meaning: reject someone
The manager turned down two people who applied for the job.
My brother asked his boss for Friday off, but she turned him down.
 

28. turn off

Meaning: stop a machine or light
Turn off the stove when the soup boils.
The blender is so noisy! Please turn it off.
 

29. turn on

Meaning: start a machine or light
Turn on the headlights. It's getting dark.
Where is the heater? I'll turn it on.
 

30. use up

Meaning: consume something completely
Who used up all the hot water?
We had a big bag of rice, but we used it up last week.
 

31. wake up

Meaning: rouse someone from sleep
The garbage truck woke up the whole street.
Grandpa is napping. Don't wake him up.
 

32. write down

Meaning: write something on paper so you remember it
Write down the address of the party.
That's a good idea. Let me write it down.

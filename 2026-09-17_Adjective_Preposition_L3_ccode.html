Adjective + Preposition

afraid of, excited about, good at, and more

Level 3 — Grammar Lesson

 

Core Idea

Many adjectives come with a partner preposition. The adjective chooses the preposition, so learn them as a pair.

Pattern: be + adjective + preposition + noun
or: be + adjective + preposition + verb-ing
Paulo is afraid of heights.
We are excited about summer.
Kate is interested in photography.

Other verbs work too: feel, look, get, become. “The dog looks jealous of the new kitten.”

 

Group 1 — of, about, with

PrepositionAdjectivesExample
ofafraid, scared, proud, tired, full, jealousThe kids are scared of thunder.
aboutexcited, worried, nervous, upsetLisa is upset about the long wait.
withbored, happy, pleased, satisfiedThe boss is pleased with the new schedule.
Tip: use about for the thing you think or worry about: excited, worried, nervous, upset.
 

Exercise 1 — of, about, or with?

Write of, about, or with. Then click to check.

1. The new bus driver was nervous ______ the icy roads.

about — nervous about + something that worries you.

2. The refrigerator is full ______ leftovers.

of — full of + what is inside.

3. Our customers were satisfied ______ the fast delivery.

with — satisfied with + a result or service.

4. Farmers are worried ______ the dry weather.

about — worried about + a problem.

5. The team felt proud ______ its first win.

of — proud of + something good.

6. Tom got bored ______ the long movie and fell asleep.

with (or of) — bored with is most common; bored of is also used in speaking.

Exercise 2 — at, in, for, to, or from?

Write at, in, for, to, or from. Then click to check.

1. That singer became famous ______ one sad love song.

for — famous for + the reason people know you.

2. Why were you absent ______ the meeting?

from — absent from + the place or event you missed.

3. Are you good ______ chess?

at — good at + a skill or subject.

4. Her handwriting looks similar ______ her mother’s.

to — similar to + the thing you compare.

5. Each student is responsible ______ one part of the project.

for — responsible for + a job or duty.

6. My sister is interested ______ becoming a pilot.

in — interested in + noun or verb-ing.

7. Don’t be late ______ your first day at work!

for (or to) — late for is most common; late to is also used in American English.

 

After the Preposition

After the preposition, use a noun, a pronoun, or a verb + -ing.

noun: I’m tired of rain.
pronoun: She is jealous of him.
verb-ing: They are nervous about flying.
Watch out: no base verb after the preposition.
✗ My son is afraid of swim. ✓ My son is afraid of swimming.

Exercise 4 — Fix the Mistake

The CAPITALIZED preposition is wrong. Say the correct preposition. Then click to check.

1. Why is your cat afraid AT the vacuum cleaner?

of — afraid of

2. Sara is interested FOR joining the soccer team.

in — interested in + verb-ing

3. I’m really tired WITH this cold weather.

of — tired of = I don’t want any more of it

4. Were you pleased OF the results of your blood test?

with (or about, by) — pleased with

5. Who in your family is good IN cooking?

at — good at + verb-ing

6. Is Spanish very different OF Portuguese?

from (or than) — different from

7. Why is Maria upset OF her test score?

about (or by, over) — upset about + a thing

 

Exercise 5 — Mixed Practice

Complete each sentence with a phrase from the box. Use each phrase one time.

proud of · worried about · responsible for · interested in · similar to · absent from

1. The coach is ______ the team’s equipment. It is his job to keep it clean.

responsible for — it is his job.

2. My twin brother and I are ______ each other in many ways.

similar to — twins are alike.

3. My boss is rarely ______ the office.

absent from — she is almost always there.

4. Grandpa is ______ his old army photos. He shows them to everyone.

proud of — he feels good about them.

5. Are you ______ a free computer class? There is one on Saturday.

interested in — you might want to join.

6. Dad is ______ the car. It makes a strange noise.

worried about — a strange noise is a problem.

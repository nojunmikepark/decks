Grammar · Level 3 · Verb Tenses

Past Continuous &
Future Continuous

What were you doing? What will you be doing?

was / were + -ing  ·  will be + -ing
 

Part 1 · The idea

In the Middle of an Action

Both tenses show an action in progress at one time. It started before that time and kept going after it.

At 6:00 p.m. yesterday, Grace was folding laundry.
At 6:00 p.m. tomorrow, Grace will be decorating the hall for her son's party.
 

Part 2 · The past

was / were + verb-ing

SubjectYes (+)No (−)Question (?)
II was sneezing.I wasn't sneezing.Was I sneezing?
he / she / itShe was sneezing.She wasn't sneezing.Was she sneezing?
you / we / theyThey were sneezing.They weren't sneezing.Were they sneezing?
Time words: at 7:00 last night   this time yesterday   when   while
 

Part 2 · Two actions

Long Action + Short Action

The long action uses the past continuous. The short action uses the simple past.

Nate was rinsing the dishes when the water turned cold.
While the band was tuning its guitars, the crowd arrived.
Tip: While goes with the long action. When usually goes with the short action. When can also join two past continuous actions that were happening at the same time: When we were hanging the lights, the kids were wrapping presents.
 

Part 3 · The future

will be + verb-ing

SubjectYes (+)No (−)Question (?)
all subjectsShe'll be snoring.She won't be snoring.Will she be snoring?

Good news: will be is the same for every subject. No was / were choice!

Time words: at 7:00 tomorrow   this time next week   in two hours   tonight at 9:00
At 10:00 tonight, the city will be setting off fireworks.
 

Part 3 · Two parts in the future

Future Continuous + when / while / and

ConnectorOther partExample
whensimple presentWhen your taxi pulls up, I'll be locking the door.
whilesimple present or present continuousWhile Dad grills (or is grilling), we'll be setting the table.
and / but / sofuture continuousOn Sunday, Omar will be fixing the roof, and his wife will be planting flowers.
Watch out: no will after when or while. Say "When your taxi pulls up," not "When your taxi will pull up."

Practice 2

Find and Fix

Each sentence has one mistake in CAPITAL letters. Say the correct sentence, then click to check.

1. The students WAS laughing at the teacher's joke.

The students were laughing at the teacher's joke. — The students = they: were.

2. This time tomorrow, we will be CAMP by the lake.

This time tomorrow, we will be camping by the lake. — will be + verb-ing.

3. While Hana was CHATING with a friend, she lost her ring.

While Hana was chatting with a friend, she lost her ring. — chat: consonant + vowel + consonant, so double the t.

4. WERE Kevin exercising when you called?

Was Kevin exercising when you called? — Kevin = he: Was ... ?

5. My boss won't be ATTEND the meeting tomorrow.

My boss won't be attending the meeting tomorrow. — won't be + verb-ing.

6. When I got home yesterday, my roommate WILL BE sewing a button on his coat.

When I got home yesterday, my roommate was sewing a button on his coat. — "Yesterday" = past: was + verb-ing.

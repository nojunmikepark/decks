Equatives: as ... as

The same or not the same?

Level 2 — Grammar

 

The Idea: The Same or Not the Same?

Mia is 12 years old. Tina is 12 years old.
Mia is as young as Tina.  (=)
Mia is 12 years old. Her cousin Sara is 9 years old.
Mia is not as young as Sara.  (≠)

as ... as = the same.   not as ... as = not the same.

 

as + adjective + as

Pattern: subject + be + as + adjective + as + noun
Your garden is as pretty as a park.
Carlos is as smart as his sister.
Tuesday was as cold as Monday.
This museum is as exciting as the zoo.
Watch out: use the simple adjective. as cold as ✓   as colder as ✗   as more exciting as ✗   as cold than ✗

not as + adjective + as

Pattern: subject + be + not as + adjective + as + noun
is not = isn't   are not = aren't
My old laptop is not as fast as my new laptop. (The new laptop is faster.)
Paul isn't as tired as Maria.
These jeans aren't as tight as those jeans.
A mouse is not as large as a horse.
Watch out: not goes before the first as. not as fast as ✓   as not fast as ✗
 

Exercise 2 — What Does It Mean?

Read the sentence. Answer the question. Then click to check.

1. My pencil is not as sharp as your pencil. Which pencil is sharper?

Your pencil. — not as sharp → the other one is sharper.

2. Kevin is not as hungry as his son. Who is hungrier?

His son. — not as hungry → the other person is hungrier.

3. The blue car is as fast as the red car. Which car is faster?

Neither. They are the same. — as ... as means the same.

4. The front yard is not as dirty as the backyard. Which yard is dirtier?

The backyard. — not as dirty → the other one is dirtier.

5. Lily's story is as short as Omar's story. Which story is shorter?

Neither. They are the same. — as ... as means the same.

6. Downtown is not as safe as my neighborhood. Which place is safer?

My neighborhood. — not as safe → the other one is safer.

Exercise 4 — Find the Mistake

The CAPITALIZED word is wrong. Say the correct word. Then click to check.

1. The kids are as NOISIER as the dogs.

as noisy as — no -er inside as ... as.

as difficult as — use as two times, not than.

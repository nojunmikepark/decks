Adverbs of Frequency

with the Simple Present

Level 2 — Grammar

 

How Often?

An adverb of frequency answers the question How often?

AdverbHow often?Example
always100%Sam always reads at lunch.
usually90%Sam usually reads at lunch.
often70%Sam often reads at lunch.
sometimes50%Sam sometimes reads at lunch.
rarely10%Sam rarely reads at lunch.
never0%Sam never reads at lunch.
 

Where Does the Adverb Go?

Main verb: subject + adverb + verb
be: subject + am / is / are + adverb
Sam often reads at lunch.
Sam is always happy at lunch.
never = 0%. Use a positive verb.
✗ Sam doesn't never read.   ✓ Sam never reads.
Questions: Do / Does + subject + adverb + verb?
Do you usually read at lunch?  ·  Do you ever read at lunch? (ever = at any time)
Am / Is / Are + subject + adverb …?  ·  Is Sam usually happy at lunch?

The adverb does not change the verb: he reads → he often reads.

Exercise 2 — Put the Words in Order

Write the sentence. Then click Show Answer.

1. always / Joon / sings / in the car

Joon always sings in the car. — adverb before the main verb

2. is / Nina's office / quiet / always

Nina's office is always quiet. (or Is Nina's office always quiet?) — adverb after is

3. never / my nephews / breakfast / eat

My nephews never eat breakfast. — never + a positive verb

4. you / do / ever / camping / go

Do you ever go camping? — Do + subject + ever + verb?

5. rarely / is / Coach Rivera / angry

Coach Rivera is rarely angry. (or Is Coach Rivera rarely angry?) — adverb after is

6. rarely / Elena / works / from home

Elena rarely works from home. — adverb before the main verb; Elena = she, so works

Simple Past

Finished actions in the past. Regular verbs end in -ed. Irregular verbs have a special past form.

Grammar Notes

1. When do we use it?

For actions that started and finished in the past.

She walked to work. They lived in Ohio.

2. Was and were

I / he / she / it + was (wasn't)

you / we / they + were (weren't)

He was late. They weren't home.

3. Regular verbs: + -ed

  • Most verbs: + ed (help → helped)
  • Ends in -e: + d (like → liked)
  • Consonant + y: y → ied (carry → carried)
  • Vowel + y: + ed (stay → stayed)
  • One-syllable verb ending in consonant + vowel + consonant (not w, x, y): double the last letter (shop → shopped; but mix → mixed)

4. Irregular verbs

Learn the past form: go → went, eat → ate, have → had, come → came, do → did

5. Negatives and questions

didn't + base verb

We didn't eat. (not didn't ate)

Did + subject + base verb?

Did they come? Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.

6. Time words

  • yesterday: yesterday, yesterday evening
  • last: last night, last month, last summer
  • ago: two days ago, an hour ago

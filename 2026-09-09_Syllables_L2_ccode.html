Syllables

Count the beats in a word

Level 2

 

What is a syllable?

A syllable is one beat in a word. Every syllable has one vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u).

Rule: Say the word slowly. Count the beats. One beat = one syllable.
cat — 1 beat — 1 syllable
hap · py — 2 beats — 2 syllables
beau · ti · ful — 3 beats — 3 syllables
in · for · ma · tion — 4 beats — 4 syllables

Say each word with the class. Clap one time for each beat.

 

Three ways to count

WayHowTry it
1. ClapSay the word. Clap one time for each beat.dog (clap) = 1  ·  wa · ter (clap, clap) = 2
2. ChinPut your hand under your chin. Say the word. Your chin goes down one time for each syllable.spa · ghet · ti = 3
3. Vowel soundsCount the vowel SOUNDS you hear. Not the letters!cake = 1 sound (the e is silent)
Quick check: Every word has at least one syllable. If you hear one beat, the answer is 1.
 

Two careful rules

Silent e: An e at the end of a word is often silent. It is NOT a syllable.
cake = 1  ·  late = 1  ·  huge = 1
-le at the end: Words that end in -le have one more beat. The -le IS a syllable.
ta · ble = 2  ·  lit · tle = 2  ·  can · dle = 2

Say "late" and "little." Feel the difference under your chin.

Why syllables? Look ahead

Next we will compare things: My car is older than your car. The number of syllables in an adjective tells us how to change it.

Adjective groupExampleNext lesson

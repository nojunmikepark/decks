Connectors

and · but · so · because · because of

Level 3 — Grammar Review

 

What Is a Connector?

A connector joins two ideas in one sentence. Each connector shows how the ideas go together.

and = more information  ·  but = a contrast  ·  so = a result
because / because of = a reason

Ask: Is the second idea MORE, DIFFERENT, a RESULT, or a REASON?

 

and · but · so

Kenji ordered a pizza and watched a movie. (more information)
The shoes were on sale, but they were too small. (contrast)
The sale ended yesterday, so the price went up. (result)
Tip: Put a comma before but and so when they join two complete sentences.
 

because vs. because of

because + subject + verb
because of + noun
Ms. Ito is happy because she won a prize.
Ms. Ito is happy because of the prize.
Because of the prize, Ms. Ito is happy. (reason first + comma)
Watch out: because of she won → because she won  ·  because the prize → because of the prize

Exercise 2 — because or because of?

Say because or because of. Then click to check.

1. The bus driver stopped ____ a cat in the road.

because of — a cat = a noun.

2. Abel's dog barks at night ____ it is afraid of the dark.

because — it is = subject + verb.

3. Lin got the best seat ____ she arrived first.

because — she arrived = subject + verb.

4. The rice burned ____ my mistake with the timer.

because of — my mistake = a noun.

5. ____ the loud fireworks, our cat hid under the bed.

Because of — the loud fireworks = a noun.

6. Omar takes vitamins every day ____ his doctor told him to.

because — his doctor told = subject + verb.

 

Exercise 3 — Fix the Mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Correct the CAPITALIZED word or words. Then click to check.

1. BECAUSE OF Nora loves music, she joined the church choir.

Because — Nora loves = subject + verb: Because Nora loves music, she joined the church choir.

2. The kids got extra recess BECAUSE their good behavior.

because of — their good behavior = a noun: The kids got extra recess because of their good behavior.

3. Mila's apartment is tiny, SO she has room for a piano.

but — A contrast: Mila's apartment is tiny, but she has room for a piano.

4. My glasses broke, BECAUSE I wore my old pair to work.

so (or but) — A result: My glasses broke, so I wore my old pair to work.

5. Mr. Lopez walks to work BECAUSE OF he enjoys the fresh air.

because — he enjoys = subject + verb: Mr. Lopez walks to work because he enjoys the fresh air.

6. BECAUSE the long line, we skipped the coffee shop.

Because of — the long line = a noun: Because of the long line, we skipped the coffee shop.

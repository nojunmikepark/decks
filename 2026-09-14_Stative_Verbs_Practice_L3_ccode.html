Stative Verbs: State or Action?

One verb, two meanings

Level 3 — Grammar Practice

 

One Verb, Two Meanings

VerbSTATE — simple presentACTION — am / is / are + verb-ing
haveI have a car.
= I own it.		I am having dinner.
= I am eating it now.
thinkI think she is smart.
= my opinion		I am thinking about buying a new car.
= my mind is working on it now
STATE meaning: what you own, your opinion, or what something is like → simple present
ACTION meaning: something a person does on purpose, happening now → am / is / are + verb-ing

Same verb. The meaning decides the form.

taste, smell, look, feel

VerbSTATE: the thing has a qualityACTION: a person does it on purpose
tasteHoney tastes sweet.Grandpa is tasting the pie.
smellFresh popcorn smells amazing.My dog is smelling the grass.
lookYour garden looks lovely.Tom is looking at the clock.
feelWool feels itchy.The mom is feeling her son's forehead.
Quick check: thing + verb + adjective = state.
a person + verb + something, on purpose, now = action.
 

Exercise 2 — Sense Verbs

Say the simple present or present continuous form. Then click to check.

1. Your shampoo ____ (smell) like apples.

smells — the shampoo has a smell, a state.

2. Right now, the tailor ____ (feel) the cloth to check its quality.

is feeling — touching on purpose, happening now.

3. At the moment, Jamal ____ (taste) his homemade salsa.

is tasting — trying the flavor, happening now.

4. That jacket ____ (look) expensive.

looks — look = seem, a state.

5. The kitchen floor ____ (feel) sticky.

feels — the floor has a texture, a state.

6. Put that down! You ____ (look) at your phone while you drive!

are looking — using your eyes on purpose, happening now.

 

Now or Every Day?

An ACTION meaning happening now → am / is / are + verb-ing
An ACTION meaning as a habit → simple present
We have dinner at seven every night. (habit)
I think about my family every day. (habit)
Look for now words: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
 

Exercise 3 — Correct the Verb

The CAPITALIZED verb is wrong. Say the correct form. Then click to check.

1. Right now, my cousins HAVE a picnic at the beach.

are having — have a picnic = an activity, happening now.

2. Sam IS HAVING a new job at the bank.

has — have a job = a state.

3. My uncle IS THINKING soccer is more exciting than baseball.

thinks — an opinion, a state.

4. Look! The police dog SMELLS the suitcases.

is smelling — sniffing on purpose, happening now.

5. They ARE HAVING a house in Mexico.

have — have a house = own it, a state.

6. Right now, Dad THINKS about a gift for Mom.

is thinking — think about = using his mind, happening now.

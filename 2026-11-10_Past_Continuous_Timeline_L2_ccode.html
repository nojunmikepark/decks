Grammar · Level 2 · Verb Tenses

Past Continuous

An action in progress at a time in the past

was / were + verb-ing
 

Part 1 · The idea

A continuous action

The past continuous shows an action in progress at a time in the past.

What was Sam doing at 6:30? He was cooking dinner.
 

Part 1 · The form

was / were + verb-ing

SubjectYes (+)No (−)Question (?)
I / he / she / itHe was studying.He wasn't studying.Was he studying?
you / we / theyThey were studying.They weren't studying.Were they studying?
-ing spelling: close → closing  ·  stop → stopping  ·  study → studying
 

Part 2 · Long action + short action

when / while

Sam was cooking dinner when the phone rang.
While Sam was cooking dinner, the phone rang.

Practice 2 · 1–2

Fill in the Blank

Say the past continuous of the verb, then click to check.

1. Yesterday at sunrise, two fishermen ____ (pull) in their nets.

Yesterday at sunrise, two fishermen were pulling in their nets. — Two fishermen = they: were + pulling.

2. The new worker ____ (not / wear) gloves when he cut his hand.

The new worker wasn't wearing gloves when he cut his hand. — He: wasn't (was not) + wearing.

 

Practice 2 · 3–4

Fill in the Blank

Say the past continuous of the verb, then click to check.

3. ____ (your boss / smile) when you gave her the report?

Was your boss smiling when you gave her the report? — Question: Was + your boss + smiling?

4. We ____ (ride) the subway downtown when the train suddenly stopped.

We were riding the subway downtown when the train suddenly stopped. — ride → riding: drop the e.

 

Practice 2 · 5–6

Fill in the Blank

Say the past continuous of the verb, then click to check.

5. When I walked into her office, Ms. Lopez ____ (plan) her vacation on the computer.

When I walked into her office, Ms. Lopez was planning her vacation on the computer. — plan → planning: double the n.

6. While the barber ____ (cut) my hair, we talked about soccer.

While the barber was cutting my hair, we talked about soccer. — cut → cutting: double the t.

Find and Fix

2. The tourists were LOOK at the bridge when the sun went down.

The tourists were looking at the bridge when the sun went down. — was / were + verb-ing.

Modals: Combined Review

What does the modal mean?

meaning first
 

Part 1 · Advice and need

Maybe, a good idea, or necessary?

MeaningModalsExample
maybemight / might notOur cat might be under the bed.
good ideashouldYou should call your aunt.
bad ideashouldn'tYou shouldn't skip breakfast.
necessarymust / have to / has to / have got toI have to mop the floor.
not necessarydon't have to / doesn't have toYou don't have to knock. Come in!
 

Part 2 · Rules and ability

Not allowed, or able?

MeaningModalsExample
not allowedmust not / can'tYou can't fish in this pond.
able (now)can / be able toNell can draw horses.
able in the pastcould / was (were) able toAt six, Rob could spell his name.
able in the futurewill be able toSoon, the baby will be able to crawl.
Watch out: say must not, not mustn't. could here = able in the past.

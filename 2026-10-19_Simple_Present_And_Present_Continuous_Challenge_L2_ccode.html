Simple Present and Present Continuous

Challenge Review — then on to the Simple Past

Level 2 — Grammar Review

 

Simple Present — Habits, Routines, Facts

Form: I / you / we / they + verb  ·  he / she / it + verb-s
Negative: don't / doesn't + verb  ·  Question: Do / Does + subject + verb?
Lucia drives to work every day.
He doesn't drive at night.
Does your cousin sing in a choir?
Hard part 1 — the subject: my mom and dad = they · one of my cousins = he / she · everybody / nobody = he / she / it
Hard part 2 — spelling: wash → washes · fix → fixes · carry → carries · play → plays

Time words: every day, usually, always, often, never, on Mondays

6. Nobody in my family ever ____ meat.

 

Present Continuous — Happening Now

Form: am / is / are + verb-ing
Negative: am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing  ·  Question: Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?
The kids are riding their bikes right now.
She isn't sleeping. She is reading.
Spelling: ride → riding · stop → stopping · sit → sitting · tie → tying
Stative verbs (thinking, feeling, owning, the senses) usually do NOT take -ing: know, believe, hate, prefer, agree.
✗ I am knowing her.   ✓ I know her.

Time words: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!

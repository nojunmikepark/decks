Parts of Speech: Practice

Nouns and pronouns, verbs, articles, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions and conjunctions.

Grammar Notes

Noun or pronoun

A noun is a person, place, thing or idea. A pronoun takes the place of a noun: I, you, he, she, it, we, they, me, him, her, them.

Maria lost her phone. She is upset.

Verb

An action or a state: swim, cook, is, have.

The kids swim every summer.

Article

a, an, the. Use an before a vowel sound.

a lemon, an egg, the moon

Adjective

Describes a noun. What kind?

a tall building / The soup is hot.

Adverb

Tells how or how often. Many end in -ly. How often: always, usually, sometimes, never.

She sings sweetly. He never smokes.

Preposition

Shows where or when: on, at, next to, behind, after.

The bank is next to the gas station.

Conjunction

Joins two ideas: and, but, or, so, because.

I called him, and he answered.

Word endings

  • noun: -ness, -ment, -th, -er (kindness, payment)
  • adjective: -ful, -ous, -al (helpful, famous)
  • adverb: -ly (calmly)
  • verb: -en, -ize (widen, organize)

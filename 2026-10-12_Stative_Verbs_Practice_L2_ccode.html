Stative Verbs

Action verbs show actions. Stative verbs show feelings, thoughts, owning, and other states. Stative verbs usually do not use -ing.

Grammar Notes

1. Action verbs

An action verb shows something you do: run, cook, write, drive.

  • Happening now: present continuous (am / is / are + verb-ing)
  • Habits and routines: simple present

Look! Mia is dancing. Mia dances every class.

2. Stative verbs

A stative verb shows a state, not an action. Some common stative verbs:

  • Feelings and wants: like, love, want, need
  • Thinking: know, believe, understand, remember, forget
  • Owning: own, belong (to)
  • Other: cost, seem

3. Use the simple present with stative verbs

Use the simple present, even with now, right now, and at the moment.

✓ Right now, I hate this rain. ✗ Right now, I am hating this rain.

4. Negatives and questions

  • Simple present (stative verbs and habits): don't / doesn't and do / does
  • Action happening now: am not / isn't / aren't and am / is / are

Does she remember me? Is she sleeping?

Tip: Ask: can I see it happening? Yes = action verb. No (a feeling, a thought, owning, or another state) = stative verb.

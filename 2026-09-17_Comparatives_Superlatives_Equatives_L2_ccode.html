Comparatives, Superlatives, and Equatives

-er than, the -est, as ... as

Level 2 — Grammar

 

Three Ways to Compare

Plant A: 20 cm.   Plant B: 20 cm.   Plant C: 35 cm.
The same: Plant A is as tall as Plant B.  (equative)
2 things: Plant C is taller than Plant A.  (comparative)
3 or more: Plant C is the tallest.  (superlative)

Look for the clue: than = comparative. the = superlative. as ... as = equative. not as ... as = not the same.

Exercise 2 — Which Form?

Say the sentence with the word in ( ). Then click to check.

1. (thick) This blanket is ____ than my jacket.

thicker — than = 2 things. 1 syllable: add -er.

2. (bright) The moon is not as ____ as the sun.

bright — not as ... as: use the simple adjective.

3. (colorful) Which bird is the ____ in the whole pet store?

most colorful — the whole store = 3 or more. Long adjective: the most.

4. (fresh) Today's bread is as ____ as yesterday's bread.

fresh — as ... as: use the simple adjective.

5. (early) Is there an ____ bus than the 8:15?

earlier — than = 2 things. Ends in -y: change y to i + -er.

6. (bad) That was ____ haircut of my life.

the worst — of my life = 3 or more. Irregular: bad → the worst.
